## Overview

This week's project was a primary research study on what drives student motivation and engagement, and what that means for the design of an educational learning platform.

The full cycle was completed within the week: survey design, distribution, response collection, data analysis, findings, and design recommendations.

---

## Contents---

## Key Findings

1. **Feedback is the strongest motivator** — 94% of respondents agreed that regular feedback would increase their motivation.
2. **Relevance drives engagement** — 87.5% lose motivation when a course feels irrelevant to their future goals.
3. **Intention doesn't translate to action** — Only 10% consistently follow through on their own study plans.

---

## Design Recommendations

1. Feedback in the core loop
2. Relevance made visible
3. Accountability scaffolding
4. Interactive-first content
5. Recognise the emotional layer

Full reasoning in `03_Report/Findings_Report.pdf`.

---

## Methodology

- **Sample size:** 16 respondents (10 for final four questions)
- **Instrument:** 15-question Google Form (Likert, multiple-choice, open-ended)
- **Analysis:** descriptive statistics + thematic coding
